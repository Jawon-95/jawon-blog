# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

This policy explains what information **JawonStudio** ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website or buy our software, and how it is used.

## 1. Code you paste into our tools

All conversions in [JS Convert Tool](/js-tool) run **locally in your browser**. The code or text you paste is **never sent to our servers** and we cannot see it.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Purchase information** — when you buy a license we receive your name, email address, country and the plan you bought from our payment processor. We **never** see or store your full card number.
- **Messages** — if you email or message us, we keep the conversation so we can help you.
- **Local storage** — the tool stores your activation key and the free-trial counter in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device.
- **Analytics & ads** — this site uses Google services (such as Google AdSense) which may set cookies to measure traffic and show ads. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. How we use information

We use your information only to deliver your license key, provide support, process refunds, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Payment processing

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Stripe. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policies, e.g. [Stripe Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

## 5. Data retention

Order records are kept as long as required for accounting and tax purposes. You can ask us to delete any other personal data at any time.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to processing. Email us to make a request.

## 7. Children

Our products are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## 8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
