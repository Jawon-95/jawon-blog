# Refund Policy

_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

We want you to be happy with what you buy from **JawonStudio**. You can try [JS Convert Tool](/js-tool) for free before buying.

## 7-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied, email us within **7 days** of purchase and we will refund the full amount — no complicated questions asked.

## After 7 days

Because the software is a digital product delivered instantly, we generally do not refund after 7 days. We will still refund if:

- the product does not work as described and we cannot fix it, or
- you were charged twice or by mistake.

## Cancellation

Plans are one-time payments and **never renew automatically**, so there is nothing to cancel. When your license period ends, simply buy again if you want to continue.

## How to request a refund

Email [topro@braveasy.com](mailto:topro@braveasy.com) with:

1. the email address used for the purchase,
2. the date and amount of the payment,
3. (optional) the reason, so we can improve.

We reply within 48 hours. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it. The activation key is deactivated once refunded.
